Simulation only. This finite, seeded matrix is not a safety certification or a statistically estimated success rate. There is no trained policy: both controllers solve online. ROS2, external worlds, camera perception, and hardware are out of scope.

Paired cases share physical world configuration, seed, fixed-index disturbance draws, tuning, constraints, horizons, initial state, and acceptance criteria. Their perceived observations can differ because their trajectories differ. Controller order alternates; cold warm-up is recorded separately in warmup.csv. Deadline outcomes depend on desktop load.

Interpretation limits

Ideal paired-line association, known pose, bounded noise, circular obstacle footprints, and a stated maximum movable-object speed are assumptions. No directional obstacle forecast is used. Complete, miss-free range observations can exclude unseen objects only while a checked future stopping tube remains inside the shrinking observed region. This is not a guarantee against arbitrary new objects spawning inside that region. With missed detections, negative confirmations are probabilistic; repeated misses can clear a real object. Conservative stale data or unknown classifications may cause a failed/stalled mission. Plant mismatch cases test limited wheelbase/acceleration errors with margins, not robust stability for arbitrary model error.

Compare progress and failures alongside speed. Fewer decision variables do not establish a runtime advantage; this matrix uses one basis/tuning and one seed, not universal superiority or multi-seed statistical evidence.
